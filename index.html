About The Event

Culturama 2026 is an annual university cultural festival organized to encourage students to showcase their talents. It provides a platform for creativity, teamwork and cultural exchange. Students from different departments can participate in various competitions and activities.

Our Activities

Dance Competition

Show your dancing skills through solo and group performances.

Singing Competition

Present your musical talent and entertain the audience with your voice.

Fashion Show

Display creativity, confidence and unique fashion styles.

Art Competition

Express your imagination through painting and creative artwork.

Photography

Capture beautiful moments and showcase your photography skills.

Drama Competition

Present creative stories and performances on stage.
